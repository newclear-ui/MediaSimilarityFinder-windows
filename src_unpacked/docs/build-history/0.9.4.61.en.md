## 1. Why the change was needed

Scrolling a similar-group list downward slowed down and jumped back to the top.
The cause was not the anchor but the destruction itself. `thumbStarved_` alone
drove `tree->clear()/grid->clear()` plus full recreation on nearly every tick,
and recreating the widget tree mid scrollbar-drag is unsafe no matter how good
the anchor restore is.

## 2. Previous structure

- `refreshStreaming()`: unconditional `rebuildGroups() + refreshGroupList()`
  (full recreation) on `force || thumbStarved_ || (new matches)`
- `groupsDirty_` cleared unconditionally at tick start, so a scroll-gated tick
  could have dropped a pending data change
- `matchSeq_/lastFillSig_` advanced only on the streaming path, so the first
  tick after a direct refill (scanFinished etc.) rebuilt redundantly

## 3. New structure

- Full rebuilds only on group data change (`groupsDirty_ || matchSeq_`
  advance). `thumbStarved_` alone never rebuilds
- `thumbCatchUpVisible()`: in-place `setIcon()` updates for visible grid items
  only. Bounded by the viewport range plus the existing thumb budget; only
  changed icons are replaced (cacheKey comparison). Tree rows carry no
  thumbnails and are skipped
- Scroll gate: no full rebuild while a scrollbar `sliderPressed` is held, plus
  a 500ms cooldown after wheel/keyboard navigation. Input is never blocked or
  eaten; only the rebuild waits for a later tick. Programmatic moves
  (`setValue`/`scrollToItem`) do not emit `sliderPressed` and never trip it
- `groupsDirty_` clears at the single `rebuildGroups()` point (direct finish
  paths included). `lastFillSig_/lastFillMs_` advance at the single
  `refreshGroupList()` point
- The 0.9.3.10 anchor code stays untouched (last-resort restore for genuinely
  structural rebuilds)
- Test-only counters `fullRebuildCount_/thumbInPlaceCount_` plus `testUiTick()`.
  Nothing exposed to telemetry/benchmark schemas

## 4. Resolved situation

- N thumbnail catch-up ticks produce 0 full rebuilds while icons still fill
  in place
- A group data change produces exactly one full rebuild (gated, throttled)
- Scroll position, selection, and widget identity survive ticks unchanged
- Ticks during a slider drag rebuild nothing and keep the dragged position

## 6. Future impact

- The mid-scroll rebuild ban is the single `scrollGateActive()` condition.
  Keep it when the middle pane changes shape
- Direct refill callers (sort/search/tab/ignore/finish) are not gated: they
  reflect explicit user actions immediately, by design. Only involuntary
  timer-path rebuilds are gated
- Observation (separate work needed, out of this build's scope): a gradient
  fixture family streaming thousands of cross-matches at once crashed after a
  fill (0xC0000005). The engine-only 240-file scan is clean, so it looks like
  a GUI-side match-storm volume issue needing its own reproduction, reduction,
  and directive. Recorded separately from the scroll regression
- Next: S4 real-screen manual acceptance (offscreen limits),
  `color_thumb` R1 (separate work)
