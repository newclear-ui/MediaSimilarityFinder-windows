# MediaSimilarityFinder v0.9.4.5 — Node B4: Stability Control

Validation baseline for B4: the brief §8 trio (moving average,
hysteresis, minimum hold) inside the scheduler. No engine or pipeline
changes. Engine 1.5.0, DB 1.0.3, cache v9, schema 1 unchanged.

## Why the change was needed
- Through B3 every observation change republished the decision each
  2 s. System noise flowed straight into the adjustment counter and
  shares, and kill/relief could flip every tick near the boundary.

## Previous structure
- `evaluate()` consumed raw inputs and published immediately.

## New structure
- SMA-4: observed rates and loads through a fixed-window average.
  Feed-if-known-else-clear (stale values never pose as fresh).
  Static baselines excluded.
- Kill-band hysteresis: kill at availability <= 0.02 (load >= 98),
  relieve above 0.05 (load < 95), keep the previous published state
  between. Integer nvidia-smi readings dither exactly across this band
  under saturation; without it the kill flips every cadence tick.
- Minimum hold (default 10 s, settable, 0 disables): the whole published
  decision moves only after expiry. Telemetry inputs (`lastHw_`) stay
  fresh; the policy holds — observation truth and policy stability split.
- First change after `decide()` is hold-exempt (B1-compatible).
  adjustments counts publishes, so the counter stops jittering.
- No engine changes: the gate reads `lastDecision()`, i.e. the published
  decision, so execution stability is inherited automatically.

## Why this shape
- The three mechanisms split one goal (rare direction changes): SMA for
  input noise, the band for boundary dithering, hold for publish rate.
  No single one stops integer-reading flips on a 2 s cadence.
- The 10 s hold default is 5x the cadence — absorbs one-or-two-tick
  spikes while delaying real transitions by at most 10 s. B6 policies may
  tune it per mode.

## Resolved state
- Published decisions are stable. B5 only adds cost inputs.

## Accuracy impact
- None. Gate positions and verdict paths untouched. Hold only delays
  publication. Identical `scan_workflow_test` on both trees (scans run
  shorter than the cadence, hold-independent).

## Compatibility impact
- Benchmark JSON keys unchanged, schemaVersion kept.
