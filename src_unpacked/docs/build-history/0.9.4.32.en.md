## 1. Purpose · Hypothesis · Target

- Purpose: investigate the accuracy divergence of the candidate DEFERRED in
  v0.9.4.31. The performance gain is already observed, so this step asks only
  "why do the outputs differ".
- Hypothesis: the divergence comes from the structural difference between
  one-step and two-step Fant resampling chains, amplified on tiny
  bilevel/edge-heavy images.
- Target: flip reproduction, score decomposition, resampling-path audit,
  R stability, pixel statistics.

## 8. Resampling-Path Audit (source-based)

```text
baseline : WIC decode -> direct target size (1 Fant step)
candidate: WIC decode -> intermediate resolution (Fant) -> in-memory Fant -> final f/a
```

- The WIC scaler is Fant in all three decode paths
  (`src/image_decoder.cpp:220,294,344`).
- `centerCropResize` is integer nearest-neighbor
  (`src/crop_fingerprint.cpp:12`).
- The candidate quantizes the intermediate image to 8-bit GrayImage and then
  resamples again, so the second Fant step sees quantized input. That is the
  structural point where the chains diverge (§9 Case B mechanism).

## 10. Case Split Results

- Case A (geometry differs → pixels differ): 0–93 files per R (§9). Compared
  separately since pixel comparison across geometries is meaningless.
- Scoring crops (`centerCropResize`, integer nearest-neighbor): only
  12–69 of 2547 crops per R stay identical (R128 69, R192 12, R256 69,
  R384 12, R512 12). Since the crop is exact-integer rather than Fant, this
  isolates the crop stage as a consequence of the pixel divergence, not an
  independent error source.
- Case B (geometry equal → pixels differ): dominant. Flip files, full sweep
  R∈{128,192,256,288,320,352,384,512}:
  hpredict f diff 2140–2676/4096 (52–65 %), max 124–216, mean 4.04–7.00;
  l1 f diff 3801–4054/4096 (93–99 %), max 63–107, mean 17.89–33.36.
  Diff count and mean grow monotonically with R on the flip files (upsampling
  invention → downsample aliasing). The whole-dataset `f ident` counts (§9/§11
  runs) are lower than these because they aggregate all formats.

## 15. Full-Groups Feasibility Conclusion

Full-scan groups comparison requires candidate buffers inside
`verifyImagePair`→`verifyBuffersFor`→scan — product-call-path changes or a
parallel full reimplementation of the scan. Cost is high, and sampled verdicts
(1691 pairs × 5 R, 0 flips) suffice for the DEFERRED verdict. **Not built.**
Recorded as a completed necessity judgement.

## 16. Answers to Three Questions + Verdict

- Q1: divergence points = two-step Fant chain + intermediate 8-bit
  quantization + nearest-neighbor crops. Specified numerically (§7, §10, §12).
- Q2: algorithm (Fant vs nearest) + resolution (intermediate upsampling
  invention) + step count (2 vs 1) combined. No single cause.
- Q3: the R-boundary flips were artifacts; pure pairs show no boundary.
- Q4: amplified on tiny bilevel/edge images (32×32, 100×100 1-bit); EXIF
  uninvolved.
- Q5: no resolution restores byte equality (chains differ inherently);
  adoption impossible without verdict-band policy.

```text

DEFERRED (verdict-band policy, fixtures, scan driver already recorded as
revisit conditions in v0.9.4.31 §9 — this document reinforces the evidence)

```
